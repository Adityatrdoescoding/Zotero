Shades of Gray

Shades of gray consist of both achromatic grayscale shades, which are varying combinations of black and white, and close colors that have very low saturation. Achromatic grays include shades like gainsboro and silver, with no saturation at all, while grayish colors include taupe, ash, and cadet gray.
